A Structured 5-Step Process

FROM YOUR OLD DOOR TO YOUR NEW BEGINNING.

Every move follows a transparent, coordinated sequence so you always know what happens next.

01

Pre-Move Survey

Detailed physical or digital item tally to calculate volume, packing needs, and carrier size.

02

Packing & Prep

Multi-ply carton packing, edge cushioning, bubble protection, and room-by-room numbering.

03

Loading & Transit

Strategic stacking inside weatherproof containers with secure cargo straps and transit lock.

04

Delivery & Unload

Punctual arrival at your destination, floor-by-floor offloading, and room-specific box distribution.

05

Unpack & Settle

Bed and desk reassembly, major appliance placement, and complete packing material clearance.
