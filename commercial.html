Logistics Framework

THE 6-PHASE OFFICE TRANSITION MODEL

How our coordinators orchestrate corporate shifting from preliminary site audit to post-move day-one floor readiness.

Phase 01

Planning & Coordination

Joint site walkaround with facility managers, property management elevator access permits, and timeline alignment.

Phase 02

Asset Inventory

Comprehensive count of all workstations, executive suites, server accessories, and pantry appliances into a unified manifest.

Phase 03

Department-Wise Packing

Color-coded boxing for Finance, HR, Sales, and Engineering to prevent mixed boxes during rapid destination unpacking.

Phase 04

Equipment Labelling

Barcode or numerical tags applied to every PC tower, dual monitor pair, and individual employee personal crate.

Phase 05

Movement Scheduling

Off-peak weekend transit windows engineered around commercial building freight restrictions and street parking clearances.

Phase 06

Unloading & Placement

Crates delivered directly to individual employee assigned desks as per new architectural seating schematics.

Facility Manager Toolkit

BUSINESS RELOCATION READINESS CHECKLIST

An interactive tracker for department heads and office managers preparing for an upcoming workplace relocation.

0 of 8 tasks completed 0% Complete

1. Appoint Internal Move Coordinator

Designate a single liaison between your leadership team and our moving operations supervisors.

2. Finalize New Seating & Floor Schematic

Map each employee name and desk ID on the destination floor plan for color-coded crate routing.

3. Secure Building Freight Elevator Permits

Obtain formal written elevator reservations and loading bay access from building management at both sites.

4. Complete IT Backup & Server Pre-Checks

Run full external data backups before IT technicians de-rack server hardware and network components.

5. Distribute Personal Crate Kits to Staff

Provide each employee with an individual numbered security box for desktop stationery and personal items.

6. Audit Confidential & Legal Archives

Seal physical employee files, client contracts, and finance vouchers in tamper-evident security crates.

7. Update Telecom & Utility Service Providers

Schedule internet lease-line transitions, municipal address updates, and access card keycard reprogramming.

8. Post-Move Floor Walkthrough

Conduct joint floor audit with our move supervisor to verify that all crates reached assigned desks.
