Attention to Vulnerabilities

METICULOUS HANDLING PROTOCOLS

Every category of home item faces unique transit hazards. Here is how our handlers safeguard each part of your home.

Furniture Dismantling & Reassembly

King bed frames, modular wardrobes, and complex dining tables are dismantled, labeled, and all bolts/fittings bagged securely.

Kitchen & Fragile Item Packing

Individual tissue wrapping, cellular divider inserts, and heavy-gauge bubble cushion for glassware, ceramic dinnerware, and spice jars.

Major Appliance Transportation

Refrigerators, washing machines, and dishwashers are wrapped in shock-absorbent foam sheets and kept upright throughout transport.

Ergonomic Loading & Unloading

Hand trucks, hydraulic dollies, and padded lifting straps prevent scraping across doorframes, staircases, or elevator interiors.

Unpacking & Room Settlement

Upon arrival, cartons are placed in designated rooms, beds are rebuilt for immediate sleep, and empty corrugated boxes are cleared.

Smart TV & Electronics Crating

Dedicated padded flight cases and rigid foam corners provide shock isolation for large curved OLED and LED television screens.

1. Sort & Declutter Belongings

Go room-by-room to donate, sell, or discard unused items to reduce unnecessary carton volume.

2. Separate Important Documents & Valuables

Keep passports, property deeds, bank tokens, and fine jewelry in a dedicated personal carry bag.

3. Identify & Flag Fragile Items

Group crystal, oil paintings, delicate sculptures, and antiques for our specialized crating review.

4. Prepare "First Night" Essentials Bag

Pack bed linens, toiletries, phone chargers, kettle, and change of clothes for day-one comfort.

5. Confirm Pickup & Destination Details

Recheck complete street addresses, contact phone numbers, and designated unloading spots.

6. Review Item Inventory

Compare your item count against our inventory estimator to ensure no furniture pieces are overlooked.

7. Check Building Elevator & Gate Access

Book service elevators with housing societies at both source and destination addresses.

8. Prepare Major Appliances for Transit

Defrost and wipe refrigerator dry; drain washing machine hoses and lock drum transport bolts.
